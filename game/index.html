VICTORY

THE STORM BREAKS

How to play

  • Goal: reduce the Storm Herald to 0 life before she does the same to you.
  • Ember: you gain 3 ember each turn (max 10). Every card costs ember.
  • Suits: ▲ Flame deals damage · ● Wave deals damage and heals you · ◆ Leaf deals damage and draws a card · ■ Stone gives you shield that blocks damage.
  • Surge: play two cards of the same suit in one turn and the second one gains +3 power.
  • Intent: the Herald announces her next move — shield up before a big strike, or race her when she is weak.
  • Keys: 1–5 play a card, Enter ends your turn, R restarts.
